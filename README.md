
Glorp the Martian crash landed on Earth mid-takeover and wrecked his UFO! :(

He realizes the humans are much bigger than him and uses his alien abilities to hijack a minimum wage worker at a diner. He needs to earn 500 "glorpobucks" to purchase materials and rebuild his UFO to escape. Good luck, and don't screw up :)

Controls:

WASD or Arrow Keys to move

e to interact

To cook food:
Go to the kitchen fridge and press "e" and then select the item you want. Then go to the stove and click e again. BE CAREFUL to not let you food get BURNT! Press "e" again to colect your item. If your item gets burnt, go to the top of the trash can in the bottom right of the kitchen and press "e"!

To enter the neighborhood and deliver:
To unlock neighorhood which gives you more money per delivery! You MUST buy the car and press "e" to interact with it. Then go to the house's front door and press "e" to finish the delivery!

USE THE TABLET:
The tablet lets you switch between neighborhood delivery mode (must buy car first) and in restauant mode! You will also be able to see what house ordered which item and how much time you have.

How to win/lose:
You will have to get a total of 500 dollars. IF you get  a total of 5 "OOPS" (which is mistakes) then you will lose and will have to restart from the begining.
